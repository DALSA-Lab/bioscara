\mbox{[}float\mbox{]} Gear reduction of the joint 

This is used to transform position and velocity values between in joint units and actuator (stepper) units. The sign depends on the direction the motor is mounted and is turning. Adjust such that the joint moves in the positive direction on on positive joint commands.
